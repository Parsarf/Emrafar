About Emrafar

High-quality healthcare solutions built on long-term trust.

Emrafar Co. combines healthcare distribution, diagnostic systems, and responsive product support with a customer-first standard shaped around long-term supplier and client relationships.

Emrafar Co. has been industry leaders in distribution of healthcare products. We have always strived to present the best products and offer high quality solutions with facilities which are FDA certified. We have always prided ourselves on the fact that core values of Emrafar Co. are derived from the idea of putting the customer first. Since the time that Emrafar Co. was founded, we have been consistently working to provide our customers with exceptional customer service. We believe that the only way to succeed is to establish long-lasting business relationships with our suppliers and clients. These valued relationships are based on trust in the fact that we will always be flexible to meet product needs.
